Checking editions matters

Different prints and online transcriptions may use different page numbers, numbering systems or arrangements. In finished scholarship, cite the edition used and record variants that materially affect the conclusion.

These links are a starting library, not a substitute for collating manuscripts or surveying the full range of Imāmī scholarship.
